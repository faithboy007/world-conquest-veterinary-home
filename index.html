Meet Our Chief Veterinarian

Dr. Dooyum Idowu

DVM, Chief Veterinarian

Dr. Dooyum Idowu leads our veterinary team with passion, expertise, and an unwavering commitment to animal welfare. With years of experience in veterinary medicine, she has built a reputation for providing compassionate, comprehensive care to pets of all kinds.

Her approach combines cutting-edge medical knowledge with a gentle, caring touch that puts both pets and their owners at ease. Dr. Idowu specializes in preventive care, ensuring your beloved companions live long, healthy, and happy lives.
